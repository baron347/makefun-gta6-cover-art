MAKE YOUR COVER

Drop 1–3 of your own photos here, or pick them from your machine. The app builds a multi-panel poster around your caption — no upload, no account.

Tip: upload 2–3 photos with different moods for the richest cover — a portrait, a street or night shot, and a skyline or beach. One photo works too: the app adds drawn graphic panels so it never looks like a contact sheet.
